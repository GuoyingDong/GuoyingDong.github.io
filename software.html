01

Design lattice structures

Build periodic, TPMS, Voronoi and randomized lattices. Make them conformal to a part, grade them heterogeneously, combine them with CAD bodies through Boolean operations, and export to STL for printing.

02

Run FEA

Validate designs with finite element analysis. Numerical homogenization gives the effective properties of a lattice, so large structures can be simulated without meshing every strut.

03

Optimize structures

Find where material is needed and remove the rest. Topology optimization results can drive a heterogeneous lattice map, turning an optimized density field into a printable lattice.

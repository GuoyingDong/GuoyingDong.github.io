Intelligent Design and Manufacturing Lab

~2,700 sq. ft. · NC2205, NC2405

The Intelligent Design and Manufacturing (IDM) Lab is housed in a dedicated research facility on the CU Denver Downtown Campus. It supports advanced research in additive manufacturing, materials characterization, and mechanical testing.

3D printing

  • Multiple commercial and custom-built 3D printers
  • Direct Ink Writing (DIW), Material Extrusion (ME), Stereolithography (SLA)
  • Thermosets, thermoplastics, and fiber-reinforced composites
  • Commercial Anisoprint continuous fiber printer with G-code control for programmable fiber paths

Characterization & fabrication

  • Optical light microscope (2–5×)
  • Polarized light microscope (20–50×)
  • Atomic force microscope (~10–100 nm resolution)
  • Muffle furnace for thermal processing
  • UV curing ovens and UV light systems
  • Drill press and band saw for mechanical post-processing
  • Fully equipped tool chest with diamond-tipped coring bits

Mechanical & thermal testing

  • Uniaxial mechanical tester
  • Cyclic mechanical tester
  • Dynamic mechanical analyzer (DMA)
  • Thermal chambers integrated with each system
  • Load cell range: 0.1 mN to 30 kN
  • Temperature range: −200 °C to 400 °C

These resources support multiscale experimental mechanics, thermal-mechanical research, and the development of next-generation manufacturing techniques.

Additive Manufacturing Lab

NC1809 · Renovated 2025

The Department of Mechanical Engineering Additive Manufacturing (AM) Lab is a student-run facility that supports the College of Engineering, Design, and Computing at CU Denver, providing hands-on experience in 3D printing and digital fabrication.
